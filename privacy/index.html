Brick Swarm

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

In short: Brick Swarm collects no personal data.

There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress and settings stay on your phone. Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, and we never see your payment details.

This policy covers Brick Swarm, a game for Android and iOS made by Abdullah Chauhan ("we", "us"). It explains what the game does with information about you, which is as little as a game can.

What we collect

Nothing. Brick Swarm does not collect, receive, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no sign-in and no ads, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We run no servers that the game talks to.

What stays on your device

To work, the game keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: the levels you have cleared, your stars and your best times
  • your settings: sound, vibration, the look in use, paddle speed and similar choices
  • which one-time purchases you own, so they stay unlocked

This never leaves your device and we never receive it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Purchases

Brick Swarm sells optional one-time unlocks. Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iOS) handles every purchase. When you buy, the store tells the game on your phone which item you own, so the game can unlock it; "Restore purchases" asks the store the same question again. We do not receive your name, email address, payment card or any other payment details. The store's own privacy policy covers what it collects: see Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions and network use

The game asks for no special permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. It plays offline. The only connection it makes is to the Google Play or App Store app on your device, to buy or restore purchases.

Children

Brick Swarm is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the age set by the law where they live).

Your choices and rights

Since we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can delete everything the game stores at any time by uninstalling it or clearing its data. Your purchases are tied to your Google Play or App Store account; manage them there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If the game ever needs to collect information, we will update this policy to say what and why before it does.
